Veritas Bible is an offline Korean & English Bible study app that works without an internet connection.

[ Features ]
• Full 66-book scripture in Korean (1910 revision, public domain) and English (World English Bible, public domain)
• Fast keyword search across both languages
• Per-verse highlights with color categories
• Private study notes per verse — AES-encrypted and stored only on your device
• Daily verse/chapter reading tracker with charts
• User-defined reading goal with progress meter
• Password-protected encrypted backup / restore (text payload)
• System / light / dark themes, Korean / English UI

[ Privacy ]
• Everything stays on your device.
• No ads, no analytics SDKs, no remote servers.
• Backup payloads leave the device only when you explicitly export them.

[ Scripture Sources ]
• Korean: Korean Bible 1910 (Public Domain, ebible.org)
• English: World English Bible (Public Domain, ebible.org)
