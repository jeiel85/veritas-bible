# Veritas Bible — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-05-23_

Veritas Bible ("the app") is an offline Bible study app published by the developer of the Google Play account `pedaiah85@gmail.com`.

## What data we collect

**None.**

The app does not collect, transmit, sell, share, or store any personal information on remote servers. Specifically:

- The app does not request or use your account, contacts, phone number, email, location, camera, microphone, or device identifiers.
- The app does not include third-party analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or crash-reporting SDKs.
- The app does not make outbound network calls. All Bible text is bundled inside the application package.

## What data stays on your device

The following data is stored **only on your device** in the app's private storage area, managed by the Android operating system:

- Reading progress (verses, chapters, session duration per day)
- Reading goals you set
- Highlights you place on verses
- Personal study notes you write (encrypted with AES-256 before being written to disk)
- App preferences (theme, language)

If you uninstall the app, all of this data is removed by the operating system.

## Backups you create

The app provides an optional "Export Encrypted Backup" feature. When you use it:

- The export payload is encrypted with a password **you** provide before it leaves the app.
- The encrypted payload is handed off to the system share sheet so you can place it wherever you want (your own email, your own cloud storage, etc.).
- We do not receive a copy.

## Permissions

The current build declares no runtime permissions. It does not need INTERNET, STORAGE, LOCATION, or any other dangerous permission.

## Children

Veritas Bible is suitable for all ages. Because no data is collected and there are no ads or external network calls, no special children's privacy controls are required.
